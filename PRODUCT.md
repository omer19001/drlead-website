# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Gulf business owners, marketing managers, and operations leads across Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Qatar. Primary persona: an SMB owner running a clinic, real estate agency, or service business — non-technical, results-focused, comparing tools to fix a real pain (slow lead response, scattered WhatsApp conversations, manual follow-ups). Secondary: a marketing or sales manager evaluating the platform for their team and needing to justify the purchase. Both land on this site skeptical and need to be convinced fast.

## Product Purpose

DrLead is an AI-powered CRM and omnichannel communication platform built specifically for Gulf businesses. The site exists to convert visitors into trial signups or demo bookings. Every page answers one question: "Can this platform fix my lead and communication chaos?" Success looks like a visitor who lands, recognizes their pain, sees a credible solution, and clicks Start Free Trial or Book Demo.

## Brand Personality

Powerful, trustworthy, local. DrLead should feel like the most competent person in the room — not flashy, not pushy. It earns confidence through clarity and specificity. Gulf-aware without being folkloric. Enterprise-grade without being cold. The tone is direct and results-oriented: "here is the problem, here is the proof, here is what to do next."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream: white backgrounds, blue buttons, every startup looks the same — DrLead must feel distinct and opinionated
- Aggressive sales funnel: countdown timers, excessive CTAs, manipulative urgency — trust is the primary conversion lever here, not pressure
- Corporate enterprise cold: stock photos, navy + gold, feels like IBM circa 2010 — DrLead is modern and human

## Design Principles

1. **Specificity over superlatives.** Real numbers, real client names, real use cases. Never "the best platform" — always "60% more bookings in 30 days."
2. **Earn trust before asking for action.** Every CTA section is earned by the content above it. The visitor should feel informed, not cornered.
3. **Gulf-aware, not Gulf-clichéd.** Acknowledge the regional context (Arabic, local industries, Gulf cities) through content and structure — not through decorative arabesque or stock photos of souks.
4. **Dark confidence.** The dark theme is a deliberate signal: this is a serious, premium tool. It should feel like Linear or Vercel — not because those are Western references, but because that aesthetic communicates craft and precision.
5. **Motion with purpose.** Animations exist to guide attention and reinforce hierarchy, never to entertain. If removing an animation makes the page clearer, remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum across all pages
- Arabic language support planned (RTL layout, Cairo font); v1 is English-only but structure must not break when direction flips
- High contrast ratios given the dark theme — text on dark surfaces must meet AA without relying solely on color
- Reduced motion: all Framer Motion animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
